# Bitcoin wallet observation pilot

The owner requested investigation of free, reliable direct whale tracking and addition in experimental mode if useful for daily decisions. Technical choices and shadow deployment are already authorized. No live activation is authorized for this feature.

Use Blockstream Esplora and mempool.space public GET APIs to independently cross-check three BTC addresses in Bitfinex's self-published wallet list (pinned revision 83d06bdb5f5a2ce05d5ba2db5a426dd44c9f5128, 2022-12-12). This is a historical custody-address sample, not a private-investor whale list, current proof of ownership, or complete exchange reserves.

Observe confirmed address balances (at least one confirmation), count pending transactions separately, and compare observed balances at approximately 24 hours and seven days. Require a reference within one hour of the target interval; otherwise show unknown. Report actual elapsed time. Balance differences include fees and do not prove buying, selling, or net capital inflows.

Fetch at most two pages of confirmed history per address per provider, deduplicate across addresses, normalize economic fields, require provider agreement, and display a bounded transaction sample. Transfers need six confirmations for the sample's eligible section; this is an operational filter, not absolute finality. Classify inputs/outputs relative to the published set only. Known-set internal transfers are separate; mixed inputs, missing address scripts, and coinbase transactions are ambiguous. Unlisted addresses may belong to the same custodian. A 500 BTC large-movement screen follows the historical research definition but is uncalibrated. Never treat sample totals as complete daily flows.

Transport: stdlib only, Python 3.7–3.14, HTTPS GET allowlist, no redirects, keys, environmental proxy, broadcasts, or authenticated calls. Use per-connection IPv4 resolution after target-host evidence of unavailable IPv6 routes and long automatic fallback delays; retain stock hostname/certificate verification and never change global networking. Two seconds between requests; at most 64 requests per run; persist provider-specific cooldowns after failures and 429. Reject malformed/nonfinite/oversized responses. Verify a shared six-confirmation canonical block anchor before and after collection and reject moving history/counter inconsistencies. Reject anchors changing between observations as a reorganization event; suppress daily comparisons until a later trustworthy run.

Use an isolated marked directory, SQLite history retained for eight days, atomic JSON/HTML output, and a process lock. Never access bot credentials, account state, live configuration, or other collector state. A failed run must remove usable metrics from the current snapshot, not republish stale successes as fresh data. Offline report generation must also enforce freshness.

Provide a polished responsive English report with locally embedded typography, timestamps, exact BTC values, attribution provenance, coverage limits, links to explorers, and explicit shadow status. No main decision-engine imports, config changes, model calls, notifications, or orders. Install a separate hardened oneshot/timer on hk after tests and independent review.

Research verdict: daily balance changes and large custody movements are plausible contextual observations worth a small forward experiment. Published studies are observational, often short-horizon and based on broader attributed feeds; they do not establish a profitable daily edge for these addresses. Evaluate incremental next-day return/volatility information against the existing price/volume baseline on future observations, with walk-forward validation and multiple market regimes, before proposing live integration.
